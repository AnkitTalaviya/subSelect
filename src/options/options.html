SubSelect

Interactive subtitles for online video

General

When off, SubSelect attaches nothing at all — no overlay, no listeners.

Logs detection decisions to the page console. Useful when reporting a site that doesn't work.

Language

Used to split words correctly and to pick a pronunciation voice. A site that declares its own caption language overrides this. Left on Detect automatically, the language is read from the track, from the player's own markup, or failing both, from the subtitles themselves.

Cannot be the same as the subtitle language — choosing one that is already taken swaps the two.

Ask AI

Adds an Ask AI button to the menu. It does not call an API and needs no key — it opens an assistant you are already signed into, with the word and the subtitle line already asked. You see exactly what was sent, because it is the message in your own chat history. Nothing is sent until you press the button.

Uses your own account with whichever assistant you pick. A free account is enough.

Ticking one asks Chrome for access to that site. That is what lets SubSelect notice you have it open and continue the conversation there. Until you tick one, SubSelect cannot see any of your tabs — Chrome does not show them to it.

A self-hosted Open WebUI or LibreChat, or any chat page you sign into. Put {prompt} in the URL if it accepts a question that way (https://chat.example.com/?q={prompt}); leave it out and SubSelect will open the page and type the question in, which needs the site ticked above.

Sends the question to a background tab and keeps you on the player. Nothing tells you when the answer has arrived.

{word}, {sentence}, {language} and {target} are filled in when you press the button. Anything else in braces is sent as you wrote it.

Pauses when you select a word and resumes when you clear it. Only ever resumes a video SubSelect paused itself — press play at any time and it stays out of the way. It also never starts a video in a tab you have left, so switching away and turning SubSelect off cannot set sound playing behind you.

Privacy

Stored on this device
These settings. Nothing else is written to disk. The word you currently have selected is held in memory only, so the toolbar popup can show it, and is discarded when the tab closes.
Sent off this device
Only what you ask for, to the provider you approved. With no provider set up, SubSelect makes no network requests at all. When one is set up, the text you selected — and the subtitle line around it — is sent to that provider when you press Translate or Definition, and never at any other time.
Ask AI
A separate thing from the providers above, and worth reading as one. SubSelect does not call an API and holds no key: pressing the button opens an assistant you are already signed into, with the word and the subtitle line already asked. What is sent is therefore visible to you — it is the message in your own chat history — and it goes to that company under your account and their terms, not ours. It happens only when you press the button, never on selection. Turning the button off in Ask AI above removes it entirely.
Answers shown in the panel
When the answer appears over the video, SubSelect reads that one reply out of the assistant's page after you press the button, and shows it to you. It reads nothing else from the conversation, keeps no copy, and does nothing on that site at any other moment. Switch Where the answer appears to the assistant's own tab if you would rather it never read the page at all.
Your open tabs
Ticking an assistant lets SubSelect ask Chrome one question, at the moment you press the button: do you have a tab open for that assistant? Chrome answers only for the sites you ticked. Every other tab — its address, its title, the fact that it exists — is never shown to SubSelect. That is why access is asked one assistant at a time rather than through the browser-wide permission that would otherwise be needed to read your tabs.
API keys
Kept in this browser's local storage and sent only to the endpoint you entered them for. They are never transmitted anywhere else and never leave with an export.
Saved vocabulary
Stays in this browser. It is never uploaded, and export files are generated on this device.
What SubSelect reads
Only subtitle text the player has already put on screen, or that the browser has already parsed from a caption track. It never touches DRM, encrypted streams or network traffic, and never downloads video or subtitle files.
Permissions
No site access is requested at install time. SubSelect runs automatically only on known video sites; anywhere else it stays dormant until you press Enable on this site.
