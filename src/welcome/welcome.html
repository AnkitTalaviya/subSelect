SubSelect

Click a word in any subtitle to look it up, hear it, and keep it.

How it works

  • Click a word, or drag across several, while a video plays.
  • Translate, Definition and Pronounce appear right next to it.
  • Save keeps the word with the sentence you met it in.

Before you start

Translation, definitions and recordings come from free public services. To use them, SubSelect sends the word or phrase you selected — and the subtitle line around it, which is what makes a translation accurate — to whichever service answers first:

  • api.mymemory.translated.net translation
  • lingva.ml translation, if the first is unavailable
  • *.wiktionary.org definitions and pronunciation recordings
  • api.dictionaryapi.dev definitions, if Wiktionary has no entry

Nothing is sent until you press Translate, Definition or Pronounce on a word you chose. There is no background traffic, no analytics, and no account. Saved words and settings stay in this browser and are never uploaded. Your subtitles, viewing history and the videos themselves are never read, recorded or transmitted.

These are third-party services with their own terms and privacy policies. If you would rather keep everything on this device, decline — clicking, selecting, copying and saving all work with no network at all, and you can turn lookups on later in Settings.

You're set

Open a video, turn its subtitles on, and click a word. SubSelect runs on YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hotstar, Twitch, Vimeo, Crunchyroll, Max, Apple TV+ and Plex. Anywhere else, use Enable on this site in the toolbar popup.
