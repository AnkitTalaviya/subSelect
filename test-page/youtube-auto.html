SubSelect test — YouTube-style auto-generated captions

Reproduces the two things that make auto-generated captions different from ordinary ones: the caption container is the size of the whole player rather than the text, and the text grows word by word instead of being replaced line by line. Select a word part way through a line — the highlight must survive the next word arriving. Esc clears.

Diagnostics

    If SubSelect's overlay is present but sits at the top of the video rather than on the caption, it has measured the full-size container instead of the text.
